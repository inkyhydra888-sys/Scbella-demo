The Author

The Story Behind the Pen Name

S. C. Bella is a debut indie author who writes emotionally driven romance and tragedy — stories where love is dangerous, obsession runs deep, and characters crave, break, and destroy each other in the name of love.

S Sameera — friends and family call her Sam
C Chengiah — her married last name
Bella In loving memory of her 14-year-old Jack Russell, who passed while she wrote Wanted

Her work is fueled by music, emotion, and the belief that the most unforgettable stories are the ones that leave scars.

Wanted is her debut — a softer, contemporary introduction to her world. The series escalates from here, veering into darker territory in Undone, Book Two in The Fractured Hearts Trilogy.
